Safety and Essentials

Keep your festival weekend easy with the key safety points, entry reminders and everyday items worth bringing with you.

01

On-site support

Festival staff and first aid points are available around the venue. Speak to the nearest steward if you need help.

02

Entry ready

Have your ticket, ID and any accessibility confirmation ready before you reach the entrance lanes.

03

Stay refreshed

Bring a reusable water bottle and use the refill points throughout the day, especially during busy headline sets.

04

Travel smart

Plan your route home before arriving and keep your phone charged for tickets, maps and group meetups.

Essentials checklist

  • Digital or printed ticket
  • Photo ID if required
  • Payment card or contactless device
  • Portable charger
  • Weather-ready layers
  • Small bag for daily items
